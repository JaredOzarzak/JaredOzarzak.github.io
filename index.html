About Me

Hi, I'm Jared. I'm from Columbus, Ohio, and I've been working with data for years. I started at Ohio Athletic Performance as a sports scientist and went from intern to lead coach. I tracked force plate data for 300+ athletes through VALD and into Excel, flagging when someone was fatigued or dropping off so we could address their training.

At AiKYNETIX, I tested an app's jump metric against force plates and found it was off by 21%. I documented it and got it fixed with the R&D team. In grad school, I ran motion capture, force plates, and EMG in the Padres biomechanics lab.
